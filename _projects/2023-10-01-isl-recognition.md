## Overview
This project focuses on bridging the communication gap for the hearing and speech-impaired community by developing a **Real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) Recognition System**. It uses Region-of-Interest (ROI) based CNNs to interpret hand gestures into text or speech.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Real-time Performance**: Optimized for low-latency inference on mobile and edge devices.
- **Hand Gesture Tracking**: High-accuracy ROI detection even in cluttered backgrounds.
- **Dynamic Vocabulary**: Supports a wide range of common ISL signs and phrases.

## 🚀 Technology Stack
- **Deep Learning**: PyTorch for model training and quantization.
- **Computer Vision**: MediaPipe for hand landmark detection.
- **Edge Deployment**: TensorFlow Lite for mobile integration.
